# Essence & Earth

Grow your own ores. Plant seeds that grow coal, iron, diamonds, netherite, mob drops and more, then work your way up from simple Terra crops to Divus gear.

## How it works
1. **Gather Terra Essence** from grass, ripe crops, mobs and Essence Ore.
2. **Upgrade it** through five tiers: **Terra, Aqua, Ignis, Aether and Astra**.
3. **Make seeds** at the **Infusion Altar**: a seed base on the altar, with essence and an ingredient on the four pedestals.
4. **Plant and harvest.** Ripe crops drop their essence and a seed. Craft the essence into the real items.

## What's inside
- **50+ resource crops:** ores and gems, mob drops, elements, Nether and End materials, dyes, coral, honey, sculk, experience and more.
- **Mob crops:** fill Soul Jars with a dagger to grow pig, cow, sheep and other animal seeds.
- **Essence farmland:** a chance of extra essence and seeds, which is higher when the farmland's tier matches the crop's.
- **Growth accelerators:** place one under a crop to speed it up. Higher tiers reach further.
- **Harvester:** collects and replants your farm automatically. Machine upgrades make its area bigger.
- **Essence gear:** armor, tools, sickles and hoes in every tier.
- **Augments:** 36 effects at the Tinkering Table, including Flight, Haste, Strength, and area Mining and Attack.
- **Divus:** awaken Astra gear at the Awakening Altar into the strongest tier, with two augment slots and tools that never break.
- **Essence Guide:** an in-game book that explains every crop, machine and recipe.

## Works with
- **Deepcore:** essence items show their own pictures in Deep Storage Crates, and the Geo Scanner finds Essence & Earth ores.
- **Hollowdeep:** rift bosses and elites drop essence, and vault chests can hold Cognizant Dust.
- **Wildbloom:** logs from a Wildbloom Logger can drop Terra Essence.

None of these are needed.

Inspired by Mystical Agriculture, so its players will feel at home. No code or art is copied.

## Install
Open the `.mcaddon` file, then turn on both the behavior pack and the resource pack for your world.
